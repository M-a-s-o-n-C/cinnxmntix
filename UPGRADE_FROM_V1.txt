UPGRADING FROM CINNXMN v1
=========================

1. STOP the bot.
2. Download/back up tickets.db.
3. Replace main.py and database.py with the v2 files.
4. Replace requirements.txt.
5. Keep your existing tickets.db.
6. Start the bot.

v2 automatically adds its new SQLite columns/tables.

Slash commands no longer use the /ticket prefix.

Examples:
OLD: /ticket setup
NEW: /setup

OLD: /ticket close
NEW: /close

OLD: /ticket category-add
NEW: /category-add

OLD: /panel multi
NEW: /panel-create

After startup, run /settings and then use:
/appearance
/accessibility
/automation

to configure the new v2 options.
